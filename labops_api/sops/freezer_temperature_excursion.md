# Freezer Temperature Excursion SOP

## Purpose
Protect biological samples when an unplanned rise in ultra-low freezer temperature is detected.

## Escalation Rules
- **Escalate immediately** if temperature rises above **-70°C** for more than **10 minutes**.
- Escalate if the alarm remains active after door check.
- Escalate if critical or irreplaceable samples are stored in the unit.

## Caution
Do NOT open the freezer door during an excursion unless moving samples to backup storage. Opening the door accelerates the temperature rise and worsens the event.

## Recommended Actions

1. Confirm the freezer door is fully closed and the door seal is intact.
2. Check the alarm panel — record the current temperature and alarm code.
3. Do not open the door unless moving critical samples to backup storage.
4. Identify and list all critical samples inside the unit.
5. If temperature is above -70°C for more than 10 minutes, contact Facilities immediately.
6. Move critical samples to backup Freezer D only when escalation threshold is confirmed met.
7. Check that intake vents around the unit are not obstructed.
8. Document all observations in the incident log with timestamps.
